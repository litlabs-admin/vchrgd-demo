## Educational: Home Charging Speeds. Trickle to Turbo.
Para 1 — 3-pin "granny charger": very slow (6–8 miles/hr), 24hr+ for long-range EVs; only use dedicated modern socket, never extension/multi-plugs (fire risk); keep as backup/emergency only.
Para 2 — Installed wallbox: own circuit, up to 7.4 kW (~3x faster than 3-pin), 25–30 miles/hr, full overnight charge; safer/faster/smarter with load balancing + off-peak scheduling. Ideal everyday solution.
Para 3 — Three-phase supply: 11 kW or 22 kW possible, but almost all UK homes are single-phase (limited to 7.4 kW). Upgrading to three-phase costly (often several thousand £) and usually unnecessary — 7 kW covers overnight needs.

## Tethered vs Untethered
Intro: Not sure whether to choose tethered or untethered? Depends on how you drive, where you park, how you want it to look. Both Seven Pro Tethered and Untethered share the same fast, safe, future-ready tech — only difference is how you plug in.
### Untethered — Flexibility
Clean, cable-free look; plug in your own Type 2 lead when needed.
- Flexible charging: different cable lengths / swap between vehicles
- Neat design: no permanent cable = sleek, compact
- Future-proof: works with any Type 2 EV or PHEV
- Best for: households with multiple EVs or minimalist setups
### Tethered — Convenience
Permanently attached cable — ready the moment you arrive home.
- Convenience first: just plug in and charge
- Time-saving: perfect for daily drivers
- Tidy storage: cable always available, wrapped around unit
- Best for: drivers using the same EV regularly who want the simplest solution

## Save More. When You Charge Smarter. (smart tariffs)
Intro: Choosing the right energy tariff is as important as the right charger. A smart EV tariff significantly cuts home charging cost vs public/standard single-rate.
### Integrated EV Tariffs
Connect directly to EV charger or vehicle API; supplier auto-controls charging (usually overnight) for ultra-low cost. Examples: OVO Charge Anytime, Octopus Intelligent.
### Time-of-Use (TOU) Tariffs
You decide when to charge, schedule off-peak (overnight). More control, still cheaper than flat rate. Examples: E.ON Next Drive, British Gas EV Driver.
### How Much Could You Save?
Average UK EV driver (7,400 miles/yr, DfT 2024):
- £800–£1,200/yr public charging
- £600–£800/yr single-rate home tariff
- £300–£450/yr dedicated EV smart tariff
= potential saving up to 60% vs public charging.
