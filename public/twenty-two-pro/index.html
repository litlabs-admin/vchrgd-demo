MEET THE TWENTYTWO PRO · 22KW THREE PHASE

Power. Precision. Performance.

The VCHRGD TwentyTwo Pro is the ultimate solution for workplace, destination, fleet and commercial three-phase EV charging.

The VCHRGD TwentyTwo Pro combines cutting-edge performance with a refined, modern design that enhances any workplace, fleet or destination charging environment. Its compact form factor, premium materials and discreet cable management ensure it looks as good as it performs — the ideal choice for businesses that value both aesthetics and functionality.

With integrated 4G, WiFi, RFID and Bluetooth connectivity, the TwentyTwo Pro supports seamless communication and control across single units or whole sites. Dynamic load management and OCPP 1.6J make it ready to scale from a few bays to a full fleet.

Advanced features such as PEN protection, dynamic load management and roaming data enhance safety and efficiency. RFID tap-and-charge lets you control who can charge, while a 3-year warranty provides peace of mind.

Combining cutting-edge technology with effortless functionality, this 22kW charge point is designed to meet the demands of modern electric mobility. Crafted with user experience at its core, the sleek two-tone matte and glossy black design complements any environment.

Push-fit installation simplifies setup and ensures ease of maintenance throughout its lifetime. Engineered for reliability and defined by its premium aesthetics, the VCHRGD TwentyTwo Pro sets a new standard in EV charging.
