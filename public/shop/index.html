TwentyTwo Pro.

Power. Precision. Performance.

The VCHRGD TwentyTwo Pro is built to drive fleet, workplace and destination charging forwards.

The VCHRGD TwentyTwo Pro is the ultimate solution for workplace, destination, fleet, and commercial three-phase EV charging. Combining cutting-edge technology with effortless functionality, this 22kW charge point is designed to meet the demands of modern electric mobility.

With integrated 4G, WiFi, RFID and Bluetooth connectivity, the TwentyTwo Pro ensures seamless communication and control. Advanced features such as PEN protection, dynamic load management, and roaming data enhance safety and efficiency, while a 3-year warranty provides peace of mind.

TwentyTwo Dual.

Twice the power. Zero compromise.

The TwentyTwo Dual delivers twice the charging power in a single, streamlined unit — designed to maximise efficiency, support dual EV usage, and future-proof your workplace or commercial site.

The VCHRGD TwentyTwo Dual is a commercial-grade powerhouse you can rely on 365 days a year. Engineered in the UK and built to withstand everything from torrential rain to freezing temperatures, it’s designed to perform in even the harshest British conditions — day in, day out.

Fully OCPP-compatible, the TwentyTwo Dual integrates effortlessly with your chosen back-end system or charge point operator — complete with access control, dynamic load balancing, usage reports, and remote diagnostics.

And it’s not just functional — it’s customisable too. From full vinyl wraps and branded faceplates to personalised screensavers, the TwentyTwo Dual can reflect your brand across every site.
