THE COMPANY

Who we are.

VCHRGD Technologies Ltd is a UK-based EV charge point manufacturer, supplying a fully certified range of AC charging products including 7kW, 22kW, and dual-socket 22kW solutions. Our Pro Series chargers are designed and engineered for durability, reliability, and ease of installation — even in the most challenging environments.

As an independent manufacturer, we have the flexibility to support a wide array of charging applications, with native support for OCPP (Open Charge Point Protocol), allowing effortless integration into any third-party backend or charge point operator (CPO) platform. Whether you’re running a private fleet, installing workplace chargers, or deploying public infrastructure, VCHRGD products fit right in.

SUSTAINABILITY

Our commitment to the planet.

Recycling is a crucial step in reducing waste and recovering valuable materials. By returning your VCHRGD charger through our official collection service, you can be confident it will be handled responsibly, with minimal impact on the environment.

As part of the Waste Electrical and Electronic Equipment (WEEE) Regulations, we provide customers with a free collection and recycling service for their VCHRGD chargers.
